All 24 badges and how bonus hearts work

The app · 4 min read · November 11, 2026

The SumSum Playground app has 24 badges. Some unlock after a single game and one takes a full month of daily visits. The more you collect, the bigger the head start you get.

What badges give you

8 badgesStart with an extra half heart
16 badgesStart with an extra full heart
24 badgesGolden block theme
On a 7-day streakAnother half heart

Games with hearts (Falling Problems, Balloon Pop) give you hearts. Timed games (Time Challenge, Beat the Clock, True or False) give you 3 seconds per half heart. The 60-Question Race has no bonus.

The fastest way to your first 8

  1. Play each of the six games once. That unlocks First game and Explorer.
  2. Make one of those a Ranked game for Challenger.
  3. Four more games gets you to 10 for Regular, and you will likely pass 100 correct answers for 100 solved on the way.
  4. In Practice, set Easy and addition only and play the 60-Question Race. It is the best place to go for Streak 20 and Flawless. With no mistakes you also reach Streak 50.
  5. Come back three days running for 3-day streak. That puts you past 8.

The hardest ones

Streak 100 needs 100 correct answers in a row in a single game. You have to hold your accuracy all the way, so slow down and check each answer. Speed vs accuracy helps here. 30-day streak is about habit, not skill: set a reminder and play one game a day.

Stored on your device — badges and records live on the device where the app is installed. Deleting the app deletes them too.
