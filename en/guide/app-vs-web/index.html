SumSum Playground is now an app — 7 things the app adds

The app · 4 min read · November 10, 2026

SumSum Playground is now an app. It passed App Store review on November 3, 2026 and is free on iPhone and iPad. The six games are the same as on the website, and the app adds several features on top.

Get the app — Download SumSum Playground on the App Store (iPhone and iPad, free). The Android version will follow once testing is finished.

1. Create group challenge rooms

A private leaderboard for your family, friends or coworkers. Create a room, send the link, and only the people with that link compete across all six games. Problems change every 24 hours and the ranking restarts every 3 days. Rooms are created in the app; invited players can also join on the web. See the group challenge guide.

2. 24 badges and a records page

The app keeps track of how many games you have played, how many problems you have solved and how many days in a row you have shown up. Hit a goal and a badge unlocks. Collect all 24 to unlock the golden block theme. The full list is in Badges and bonus hearts.

3. Bonus hearts

With 8 badges you start each game with an extra half heart; with 16, a full extra heart. A 7-day streak adds another half. In timed games each half heart becomes 3 seconds. It is a small reward for showing up regularly.

4. One more chance

When a game ends you can watch a short ad and continue from where you stopped, which matters most when you were just short of your best. When you tap Play again you can also choose to start with one more heart. Skip it if you prefer, and if you buy "Remove ads" once, you get the bonus with no ads at all.

5. See your own rank

The website shows the top 10. The app also shows where you stand even when you are outside it. Each player has a single row, so a better score simply replaces your old one.

6. Change your nickname

Change it in Settings and it updates in the Hall of Fame and in every group room you are in.

7. Full screen, one tap away

No address bar, so problems and the number pad are larger. Tap the icon and you are playing, which makes a one-minute game at the bus stop easy.

When the website is better

  • You want to type fast on a computer keyboard
  • You just want one quick game without installing anything
  • You are showing it on a classroom TV or whiteboard

Web and app records are stored separately. If you plan to keep playing, start in the app.
