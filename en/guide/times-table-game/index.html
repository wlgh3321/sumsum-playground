Times tables game — learn them by playing, not chanting

Find a game · 4 min read · November 11, 2026

Knowing the times tables means answering mixed-up questions instantly, not reciting them in order. Games shuffle the questions and keep them coming, which is exactly the practice multiplication facts need. Here is how to use SumSum Playground as a times tables game.

Setting it up

  1. Pick any game on the home screen.
  2. Choose Practice and switch on Multiply only.
  3. Choose a level. Easy goes up to the 5 times table, Normal to the 9s, Hard to the 12s.
  4. Choose a speed and tap Play.

You can also drag the number range slider to stop wherever you like, for example at the 7s.

Four games that suit times tables

Balloon PopFor beginners. You only choose from the answers shown.
True or FalseIs "6 × 7 = 48" right? Trains you to spot near-miss answers.
Time ChallengeHow many in a minute? Easy to compare day to day.
60-Question RaceYou type each answer. The final check that the facts are automatic.

A step-by-step order

Step 1: 2s and 5s first (Easy)

The 2s are doubling and the 5s end in 0 or 5. The patterns are visible, which builds confidence.

Step 2: up to the 9s (Normal)

Most people slow down at the 6s, 7s and 8s, especially 6 × 7, 6 × 8 and 7 × 8. Because missed problems come back more often, you will meet exactly those again and again.

Step 3: mix in division

Switch on both Multiply and Divide. Seeing 56 ÷ 7 and thinking of 7 × 8 makes the facts stick from both sides.

Step 4: up to the 12s (Hard)

The 11s and 12s help with time, dozens and quantities. Once 12 × 12 comes instantly, mental math gets noticeably easier.

Adults too

After a long break, adults stall at 7 × 8 as well. It comes back within days. Try Time Challenge on Multiply and Normal, and make 30 in a minute your first goal.
