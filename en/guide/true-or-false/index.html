True or False — spot wrong equations at a glance

Game guides · 3 min read · November 1, 2026

You see a complete equation such as "8 × 9 = 81". Tap O if it is right and X if it is wrong. On top of calculation, it trains your eye for checking answers.

How to play

  1. Choose True or False. In Practice you can set the time from 30 seconds to 2 minutes.
  2. Tap O if the equation is right, X if it is wrong.
  3. Keep going until time runs out.

Scoring

Right call10 points
Wrong call−10 points and −3 seconds

Wrong calls cost both points and time, so guessing won't raise your score.

Checking tricks

1. Look at the last digit

47 + 38 = 86 ? Ones digits: 7 + 8 = 15 → the answer ends in 5 86 ends in 6, so X

2. Even or odd

Even × anything is even, so 6 × 7 = 43 is X without calculating. Odd + odd is even, and even + odd is odd.

3. Check the size

9 × 9 = 18 is far too small and 12 + 13 = 52 is far too big. A rough estimate catches many wrong equations.

4. Check division by multiplying

For 42 ÷ 6 = 8, think 6 × 8 = 48. That isn't 42, so X.

Use it in tests — The last-digit, even/odd and estimate checks work just as well for checking your answers in a school test.

What it trains

True or False builds number sense and checking habits. It is great for students who calculate well but make frequent slips.
