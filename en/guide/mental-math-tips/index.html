7 tricks for faster mental math

Math practice tips · 5 min read · November 1, 2026

People who are fast at mental math aren't geniuses; they know a few ways to make calculations easier. Here are seven you can use today.

1. Make tens first

When adding several numbers, pair up the ones that make 10.

7 + 6 + 3 + 4 = (7 + 3) + (6 + 4) = 10 + 10 = 20

2. Add from the left

On paper we start with the ones, but in your head it is easier to start with the tens.

46 + 37 Tens: 40 + 30 = 70 Ones: 6 + 7 = 13 Together: 70 + 13 = 83

3. Round, then adjust

Numbers close to a round number, like 9, 19 or 99, are easier if you round first and fix it afterwards.

58 + 29 → 58 + 30 − 1 = 87 73 − 19 → 73 − 20 + 1 = 54

4. Subtract by adding up

Subtraction with borrowing is easier as "how much do I add?"

62 − 47 = ? 47 to 50 is 3, 50 to 62 is 12 3 + 12 = 15

5. Split multiplication

Break a two-digit number into tens and ones.

14 × 6 = 10 × 6 + 4 × 6 = 60 + 24 = 84

6. Times 5 is half of times 10

18 × 5 = 18 × 10 ÷ 2 = 180 ÷ 2 = 90

7. Turn division into multiplication

63 ÷ 7 is "7 times what is 63?" Solid times tables make division fast too.

Make the tricks stick

Reading isn't enough. Pick one trick a day and use it on purpose while playing, for example "round and adjust" during a 1 min Challenge. In a week all seven will feel natural.

Accuracy first — A trick is useless if it leads to wrong answers. Go slowly at first and only speed up once you are above 90% correct.
