1 min Challenge — solve as many as you can

Game guides · 3 min read · November 1, 2026

Answer as many problems as you can before time runs out. You pick from 12 choices instead of typing, so the faster you calculate, the faster your score climbs.

How to play

  1. Choose 1 min Challenge. In Practice you can set 30 seconds, 1 minute, 1.5 minutes or 2 minutes, and the name changes to match (for example "30 sec Challenge").
  2. A problem appears with 12 answer choices below it, sorted from smallest to largest.
  3. Tap the right one and the next problem appears instantly.

Scoring

Correct10 points
Wrong choice−5 points and that choice is disabled. The same problem stays.
Time upYour score so far is your record.

Because wrong taps cost points, tapping randomly actually lowers your score. Problems get a little harder every 10 correct answers.

Finding the answer fast

The choices are in order

Choices always run from small to large. Estimate first and look only near that value. For 38 + 47, think "a bit over 80" and check the last row first.

Check the last digit

When several choices are close, work out just the ones digit: 8 + 7 = 15, so the answer ends in 5. Only one or two choices will match.

Even or odd for multiplication

If either number is even, the product is even. 7 × 8 can't be odd, so skip every odd choice.

Ranked — Ranked 1 min Challenge uses the same rules for everyone (Normal, all four operations, 1 minute), so it is a fair way to compete with friends and family.

What it trains

1 min Challenge builds estimation and calculation speed. It suits students who know the methods but are slow, or who run out of time in tests.
