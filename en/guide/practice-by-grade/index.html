What to practise at each level, and how to set up SumSum Playground

Math practice tips · 5 min read · November 11, 2026

Arithmetic is built step by step, and a shaky lower step causes slips higher up. Here is what to practise at each level and how to set up Practice mode.

Grades are approximate. Every child moves at their own pace, so start where problems feel comfortable.

Practice by level

LevelWhat to practiseSumSum Playground settings

Grade 4+Mixed operations, fast and accurateAll four · Normal–Hard · 60-Question Race, Ranked
AdultsBrain training, keeping sharpAll four · Hard · Beat the Clock

Number ranges

  • Easy: addition and subtraction up to 10, multiplication and division up to the 5s
  • Normal: up to 20, and up to the 9s
  • Hard: up to 50, and up to the 12s

You can also set the range yourself with the sliders. An addition range of 15, for example, is perfect for a child who has just learned carrying.

Signs it is time to move up

  • Accuracy of 90% or more on the results screen three games in a row.
  • The review list is almost empty.
  • Your child says "this is too easy".

If you see two of these, raise the range or speed one step. If accuracy drops below 70%, it is fine to go back a step; that is strengthening the basics, not failing.
