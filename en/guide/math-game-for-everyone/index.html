Why this is a math game for adults too

Behind the game · 4 min read · November 11, 2026

"Math game" usually suggests homework for primary school. SumSum Playground was never built that way. A seven-year-old, a thirty-year-old and a seventy-year-old play on the same screen under the same rules.

You use arithmetic for life

Splitting a bill, working out a sale price, counting the time left. The math adults use most is still adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing. With a calculator always nearby, plenty of adults hesitate at 7 × 8. That is not lack of ability, only lack of use, and it comes back after a few games.

The rules that follow

Scores, not stickers

If "Great job!" pops up after every answer, adults close the tab. SumSum Playground speaks like an arcade game: score, record, rank. When you do well the number goes up, and that is enough.

No setup

No sign-up, no tutorial. Pick a game, tap, and it starts after 3, 2, 1. A game takes about a minute, short enough for an elevator wait.

You choose the difficulty

In Practice you pick the operations, the number range and the speed. Slow single-digit addition is fine. So is all four operations at top speed. Nothing is locked to an age.

Rankings under identical conditions

Ranked games are the same for everyone, so students and office workers sit side by side in the Hall of Fame. A child who just learned the times tables really can beat a parent.

Who it suits

  • Commuters: one game instead of the news. A minute is enough to wake your head up.
  • People studying for exams: use it for accuracy practice to cut careless errors.
  • Parents and kids: competing in the same game turns homework into play.
  • Older adults: a little daily practice reading numbers and answering quickly.
  • Friends: settle a bet with a group challenge.

If you are new, try this order

  1. Warm up with True or False. Two buttons only.
  2. See how many you get in a minute in Time Challenge.
  3. Get past level 3 in Falling Problems. That is where it becomes a real game.
  4. Once it feels familiar, post a score in Ranked.
