A guide for parents and teachers

Help · 4 min read · November 11, 2026

SumSum Playground is a free arithmetic game you can use straight away without signing up. It works well as a short daily practice at home or a five-minute warm-up at the start of a lesson.

At home

  • Short and daily: about five minutes a day is plenty. See building a five-minute habit.
  • Match the level: set the operations, number range and speed in Practice. The level guide helps.
  • Review mistakes together: after a game, look at the review list and ask "where did it get confusing?"
  • Track records: best records are saved on the device, so you can compare with a week ago.

In the classroom

  • Warm-up: put Balloon Pop or True or False on the board or TV and play together. For True or False the whole class can answer with O/X hand signs.
  • Team challenge: each team member plays one 1 min Challenge and the team adds up the scores.
  • Ranked: Ranked uses the same rules for everyone, so students can compare fairly.
  • Individual practice: on tablets, set Practice to each student's level. No install or account is needed.

Using it safely

  • There is no sign-up or login, and we never ask for names or contact details.
  • Only a nickname is entered for the Hall of Fame. Remind children to use a nickname, not their real name or school. Nicknames are limited to 8 characters and profanity is filtered.
  • If you see an inappropriate nickname, email jhl.studio.00@gmail.com and we will remove it.
  • The website may show ads. When young children play, please keep an eye out so they don't tap ads by mistake.
  • See our privacy policy for details.

Tell us what you need — If a feature would help your lessons (for example, practising a single times table), send us an email and we will consider it for a future update.
