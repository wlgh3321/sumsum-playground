Brain game — a one-minute workout with numbers

Find a game · 3 min read · November 11, 2026

A brain game does not have to be a long, hard puzzle. Doing easy calculations quickly, one after another, already keeps your head busy. A game on SumSum Playground lasts about a minute, so it fits into any gap in the day.

Each game uses something different

True or FalseSnap judgment. Decide at once whether an equation holds.
Falling ProblemsPrioritizing. Deal with the most urgent of several problems.
Balloon PopHand-eye coordination. Find and tap the answer among moving balloons.
Beat the ClockStaying calm. Avoid mistakes while the time drains away.
Time ChallengeFast scanning. Find one answer among 12.
60-Question RaceStamina. Hold the same focus from start to finish.

When to play

  • Slow morning: one game of True or False is enough.
  • Sleepy after lunch: you cannot sit still in Falling Problems.
  • Cannot settle into work: the 60-Question Race brings back the feeling of doing one thing only.
  • Winding down: Balloon Pop at a slow speed.

Same rules for everyone

SumSum Playground is not divided by age. Children, office workers and grandparents play on the same screen with the same rules. You choose the difficulty in Practice, and Ranked is identical for all. Families can compete in one room with a group challenge.

Keeping it up

Short and often beats long and rare. Attach it to a fixed moment of the day, like brushing your teeth. The app counts the days you play in a row and adds a bonus heart on a 7-day streak. More in The five-minute habit.

Note — SumSum Playground is a game played for fun. It gives you calculation practice and a minute of focus, and it is not a program for preventing or treating any condition.
