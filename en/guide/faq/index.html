Frequently asked questions

Help · 3 min read · November 11, 2026

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is free and there is no sign-up. Open the site and start playing.

Which devices does it work on?

Any phone, tablet or computer browser. On a computer you can type answers with the number keys. The iPhone and iPad app is on the App Store, and the Android app is on the way. See what the app adds.

Where are my records saved?

Best records, badges and missed problems are saved in your browser on this device, so each device has its own records. Clearing your browser's site data also clears them.

What is the difference between Practice and Ranked?

Practice lets you change the operations, number range, speed and time. Ranked uses the same rules for everyone (Normal, all four operations, normal speed, 1 minute), so you can compete fairly and put top scores in the Hall of Fame.

How do I get into the Hall of Fame?

Get a top score in Ranked and a nickname box appears on the results screen. Nicknames can use letters and numbers, up to 8 characters. Please use a nickname rather than your real name.

How do I remove my score from the Hall of Fame?

Email jhl.studio.00@gmail.com with the game and your nickname and we will remove it.

The same problems seem to come back.

That's on purpose. SumSum Playground remembers problems you missed and shows them more often until you get them right. It helps you practise your weak spots.

Are there division problems with remainders?

No. Division problems always divide evenly.

Can I switch to Korean?

Yes. Use the language buttons to switch between English and 한국어. The Korean guide is at /guide/.

How do I turn the sound off?

Use the sound and music buttons at the top of the screen to turn effects and background music off separately.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Send it to jhl.studio.00@gmail.com. Telling us the device and game helps us fix it faster.
