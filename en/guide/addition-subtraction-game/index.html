Addition and subtraction game — up to 10, 20 and 50

Find a game · 4 min read · November 11, 2026

Addition and subtraction are the base of all calculation. On SumSum Playground you can play with addition only, subtraction only, or both. What you practice changes with the number range, so choose the step that fits.

Setting it up

  1. Pick a game and choose Practice.
  2. Switch on only Add and Subtract, or just one of them.
  3. Choose a level, or drag the number range slider.

What each range practices

Up to 10 (Easy)Single-digit sums like 3 + 4 and 9 − 5. No carrying. This builds number sense.
Up to 20 (Normal)8 + 7 and 15 − 9 bring in carrying and borrowing. The step worth the most time.
Up to 50 (Hard)Two-digit sums like 27 + 18 and 43 − 26. Mental strategies start to matter.

Games for each step

Up to 10: Balloon Pop

No typing, only choosing. Set the speed to Very slow for time to think.

Up to 20: Falling Problems and Time Challenge

For 8 + 7, give 2 to the 8 to make 10, then add the remaining 5. Practice that by typing answers in Falling Problems, and check your speed in Time Challenge. The method is explained in carrying and borrowing.

Up to 50: 60-Question Race

Add the tens first. 27 + 18 is 20 + 10 = 30, then 7 + 8 = 15, so 45. After 60 of these the steps get clearly faster.

If subtraction is the hard part

It is normal for subtraction to be slower. Try two things.

  • Think of it as addition: 15 − 9 becomes "9 plus what makes 15?"
  • Play subtraction only: in a mix you score with the additions. Switch on Subtract alone and work up from Easy.

For adults

Try Beat the Clock on Hard. Keeping a run of two-digit sums going without a slip is not easy for adults either, and it speeds up estimating a total while shopping.
