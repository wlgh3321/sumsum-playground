Balloon Pop — never let the right balloon escape

Game guides · 3 min read · November 1, 2026

A problem appears and four number balloons float up. Tap the balloon with the right answer to pop it. It only takes one finger, so even young children pick it up quickly.

How to play

  1. Choose Balloon Pop and start.
  2. A problem such as 3 + 17 = ? appears, and four balloons rise at different speeds.
  3. Pop the right balloon to move on to the next problem.

Points and hearts

Pop the right balloon10 points × current level
Tap a wrong balloonLose one heart
Right balloon flies awayLose one heart and move to the next problem

You level up every 8 correct answers, and each level makes the balloons 25% faster (up to 3.5 times the starting speed). Each balloon has a slightly different speed, so the right one can escape first.

Tips

Calculate first, look second

Moving balloons pull your eyes. Work out the answer before you look at them; once you know the number, finding it takes under a second.

Watch the fastest balloon

If the right balloon is the fastest, it disappears quickly. After calculating, check the highest balloons first.

A wrong tap costs the same as a miss

Both cost one heart, so if you are unsure, take another moment to check.

Playing with young children — In Practice, choose addition only, the Easy range (up to 10) and the slowest speed. Small wins make children want to move up a level on their own.

What it trains

Balloon Pop builds quick recall. It is ideal for addition and subtraction basics and for checking times tables, and it works for children who aren't comfortable typing yet.
