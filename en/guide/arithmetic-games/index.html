Arithmetic games — 6 free games you can play right now

Find a game · 4 min read · November 11, 2026

If you are looking for arithmetic games, you can play six of them on SumSum Playground right now. They turn addition, subtraction, multiplication and division into arcade games, free, with no sign-up and nothing to install. They run on phones, tablets and computers.

The six games at a glance

Falling ProblemsType the answer before a problem reaches the floor. The level goes up every 20 correct answers.
Time ChallengePick the right answer from 12 choices before time runs out. A wrong pick costs 5 points.
60-Question RaceTimed until you get 60 right. Lower is better.
Balloon PopPop the balloon carrying the right answer as they float up.
Beat the ClockA right answer adds 2 seconds, a wrong one takes 3. See how long you last.
True or FalseTap O if the equation is right, X if it is wrong. The fastest of the six.

Which one should you play?

You just want to try it

Start with True or False. Two buttons, nothing to learn.

You want arcade tension

Falling Problems puts several problems on screen at once, so you have to decide which to clear first. Higher levels are worth more points.

You want a number for your speed

The 60-Question Race records a time, which makes it the easiest to compare with last week.

You want better accuracy

Beat the Clock punishes mistakes heavily, which is good for breaking the habit of rushing.

You are playing with a young child

Balloon Pop needs no typing, only tapping.

Choose the operations and difficulty

In Practice you can switch each of the four operations on or off. Pick Easy, Normal or Hard, or set the number range yourself.

  • Easy: addition and subtraction up to 10, multiplication and division up to the 5 times table
  • Normal: up to 20, and up to the 9 times table
  • Hard: up to 50, and up to the 12 times table

There are five speeds from Very slow to Very fast. Division problems always divide evenly.

Compete with others

Ranked mode gives everyone identical conditions. A good score puts your nickname in the Hall of Fame. To compete only with people you know, create a room with the app's group challenge.

Missed problems come back

Problems you get wrong are remembered and appear more often in later games until you get them right. Just playing gives your weak spots extra repetition.

There is an app too — get it for iPhone and iPad on the App Store. See what the app adds.
