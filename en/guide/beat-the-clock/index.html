Beat the Clock — keep the timer alive

Game guides · 3 min read · November 11, 2026

The clock keeps ticking down, but every right answer adds time. See how long you can survive.

How to play

  1. Choose Beat the Clock. The clock starts at 30 seconds.
  2. Each problem has four choices. Tap the right one to get the next problem.
  3. The game ends when the clock hits zero.

Time rules

Correct10 points and +2 seconds (shrinking as you level up, to a minimum of +1 second)
Wrong−3 seconds
Clock limitThe clock can't go above 30 seconds

You level up every 10 correct answers, and each level reduces the time bonus by 0.1 seconds. It is relaxed at first and gets tighter the longer you last.

Strategy

One mistake = 1.5 right answers

A mistake costs 3 seconds while a right answer earns 2. You need more than one and a half right answers to make up for one mistake, and three once the bonus drops to 1 second. In this game accuracy beats speed.

Slow down when the clock is full

Near 30 seconds, extra time is wasted, so focus on being correct. Below 10 seconds, clear easy problems quickly to build time back up.

Enjoy the combos

Three or more right answers in a row show a combo. Keeping the combo going naturally keeps you alive longer.

What it trains

Beat the Clock builds the habit of not making careless mistakes. It shows children who rush that being accurate is what lets you last.
