Math games to play with friends — 3 ways to compete

Find a game · 3 min read · November 11, 2026

Alone, a calculation game is practice. With other people, it is a contest. Here are the ways to compete on SumSum Playground, by situation.

1. Against everyone: Ranked and the Hall of Fame

Pick a game and start it in Ranked. Everyone gets identical conditions: Normal level, all four operations, default speed. A top score lets you put a nickname in the Hall of Fame. Each of the six games has its own board, so being good at one is enough.

2. Against people you know: group challenge

Create a room in the app and send the link. Only people with the link are ranked.

  • One room covers all six games.
  • Everyone gets the same problems for the day, so it is fair.
  • The ranking restarts every 3 days, so anyone can take first place again.
  • Invited players can join on the web without the app.

Post the link in a group chat and people will play a game when it suits them and check the standings. Details are in the group challenge guide.

3. In the same room: take turns

With one device, take turns. Use the same Practice settings and compare scores.

  • One-minute duel: Time Challenge lasts exactly a minute, so nobody waits long.
  • 60-question relay: two people alternate every 10 problems for a team time.
  • True or False with hands: put it on a big screen and have everyone signal O or X.

When skill levels differ

An adult against a child, or an expert against a beginner, stops being fun quickly. Give each player a different level in Practice, one on Hard and one on Easy, and compare the scores.

Ideas for stakes

  • Last place when the 3-day round ends buys the coffee
  • The family room winner picks dinner
  • Write each round's winner on the classroom board

Share a result — after a Ranked game you can send a challenge with your score and ask a friend to beat it.
