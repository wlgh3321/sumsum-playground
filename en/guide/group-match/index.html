Group challenge — a private leaderboard for friends and family

The app · 4 min read · November 11, 2026

The Hall of Fame is open to the whole world, so getting on it is hard. A group challenge is a small leaderboard for only the people you invite. Drop one link in your group chat and it starts.

The rules at a glance

GamesOne room covers all six games
ProblemsThey change every 24 hours from when the room was made; until then everyone gets the same ones
RankingRestarts every 3 days (72 hours). The room itself stays
ScoresPlay as often as you like; only your best counts
NicknamesEach nickname can be used by one person per room

Creating a room (app)

  1. In the app, open the Ranking tab and tap Group challenge → Create.
  2. Type a room name and your nickname, for example "Family Math Champs".
  3. Tap Create room, then use Invite friends to send the link.

You can also create a room from the result screen after a Ranked game. In that case the score you just made becomes the first record in the room.

When you are invited

Tap the link to open the room. You can join in the app if you have it, or on the web if you do not. Pick a game at the top, tap Play, and you get that room's problems. When you finish, enter a nickname to save your record. Rooms you have joined are listed in the app, most recent first.

Why do the problems change every day?

Everyone needs the same problems for it to be fair, but if they never changed, people could memorize them. So problems stay the same for a day and are replaced the next. A game started just before the change and finished after it is not recorded.

Why does the ranking restart every 3 days?

One huge score on day one can make everyone else give up. A fresh start every 3 days gives late joiners, and whoever came last, another shot at first place.

Ideas

  • Family room: one game each after dinner. Parents do lose to their kids on times tables.
  • Friends or coworkers: loser buys the coffee.
  • Class or club: treat every 3 days as a round and name a winner each time.

What the room owner can do

The person who created the room can rename it or delete it. Deleting a room removes its rankings and cannot be undone.

There are badges too — saving a record in a group challenge unlocks "Let's play", and finishing first unlocks "Room champion". Group challenges need the latest version of the app; if you do not see the menu, update the app from the store.
