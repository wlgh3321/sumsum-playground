Falling Problems — how to stop the rain

Game guides · 4 min read · November 11, 2026

Math problems fall from the sky like rain. Type each answer before the card hits the ground. It is the most arcade-like game in SumSum Playground and the one we recommend trying first.

How to play

  1. Pick Falling Problems and tap Play.
  2. After the 3-2-1 countdown, problem cards start to fall.
  3. Type the answer on the number pad (or your keyboard on a computer) and press OK.
  4. Every card with that answer pops.

You don't need to choose a card. If two cards share the same answer, one correct entry clears both.

Points and hearts

Correct10 points × current level (30 points at level 3)
Wrong answerLose half a heart
Card hits the groundLose one heart
All 3 hearts goneGame over

You level up every 20 correct answers. Each answer is worth more, but cards appear more often and fall faster. Up to 8 cards can be on screen at once.

Tips for a higher score

1. Always clear the lowest card first

Cards near the top can wait. Solving the card closest to the ground first is the single best way to keep your hearts.

2. Grab the easy ones

When several cards are at the same height, clear quick ones like 2 × 3 first. An emptier screen gives you time to think about the harder ones.

3. A wrong answer costs half, a miss costs a whole heart

Guessing costs half a heart, while letting a card land costs a full one. If a card is about to land, a confident guess is worth it. If it is still high up, take your time and calculate.

4. Hands on the pad, eyes on the screen

Looking down at the keypad for every digit lets cards sneak down. On a phone, rest both thumbs over the pad; on a computer, use the number keys.

Use Practice mode — Practice lets you choose from five falling speeds. Start slow to build accuracy, then move up one step once you rarely lose a heart.

What it trains

Falling Problems builds the habit of scanning several problems and deciding what to do first. Because you type the answer yourself, you have to finish every calculation exactly. It is great for children who have just learned carrying or their times tables.
