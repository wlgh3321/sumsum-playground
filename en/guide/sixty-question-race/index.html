60-Question Race — how to cut your time

Game guides · 3 min read · November 11, 2026

Solve 60 problems as quickly as you can. In every other game a higher score is better, but here a shorter time is the better record.

How to play

  1. Choose 60-Question Race and start.
  2. Type each answer on the number pad and press OK.
  3. A banner shows your progress every 10 problems.
  4. The clock stops the moment you answer the 60th problem correctly.

What happens when I'm wrong?

A wrong answer keeps the same problem on screen. There is no penalty, but the clock keeps running while you try again. In the end, being accurate is the fastest way. Missed problems also appear in the review list on the results screen.

Tips to cut your time

Find a rhythm

Sixty problems is longer than it sounds. Rushing the first ten usually causes mistakes later. Repeat "read → calculate → type → OK" at a steady pace.

A problem isn't done until OK

Many players type the answer and then hesitate before OK. On a phone, keep your right thumb near the OK button to save a fraction of a second on every problem. Over 60 problems that adds up.

Turn division into multiplication

Think of 56 ÷ 8 as "8 times what is 56?" and your times tables give you 7 right away.

Set a goal — Note your first time, then play once a day for a week. Cutting your time by 10% means your speed has really improved.

What it trains

Finishing a fixed set builds focus and stamina. It feels like a school worksheet, which makes it good practice before a test.
